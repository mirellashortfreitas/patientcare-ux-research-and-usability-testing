# PatientCare — UX Research & Usability Testing

## 📌 Project Overview

**PatientCare** is a university UX research and design project focused on improving the usability, accessibility, privacy, security, and user engagement of digital healthcare applications.

The project explored how a healthcare platform could provide a more accessible and user-centred experience for patients and healthcare professionals, with particular attention to elderly users, users with lower digital confidence, and people concerned about privacy and security.

**Project type:** University UX Research & Design Project
**Role:** UX Research, User-Centred Design, Prototyping & Usability Testing
**Tools:** Figma, Microsoft Forms
**Domain:** Healthcare Technology / Digital Health

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objective was to investigate common problems experienced by users of digital healthcare platforms and use the findings to develop a more accessible and user-friendly healthcare application concept.

The research focused on:

* Usability and accessibility
* User experience and engagement
* Privacy and data security concerns
* Digital exclusion
* Healthcare application navigation
* User trust and confidence
* Communication between patients and healthcare professionals

---

## 🔎 Research & Analysis

The project combined academic research with primary user research.

An online survey containing **10 questions** was completed by **11 participants aged between 18 and 70** who regularly use healthcare services.

The research identified several recurring concerns, including:

* Confusing navigation
* Technical difficulties
* Slow system performance
* Accessibility limitations
* Privacy and security concerns
* Uncertainty regarding information accuracy
* Lack of user-friendly design

### Key Research Findings

The survey indicated that:

* **6 participants** rated application navigation at **3/5**.
* **5 participants** rated the clarity of instructions at **3/5**.
* **5 participants** rated accessibility for users with different digital skill levels at **2/5**.
* Privacy and security concerns affected users' confidence in digital healthcare platforms.
* Technical difficulties and reliability issues were also identified.

These findings were used to inform the UX design process and identify areas for improvement.

## 👤 User Personas

Three user personas were developed based on the research findings.

The personas represented different user needs, behaviours, goals, and pain points when interacting with digital healthcare services.

The personas helped guide design decisions throughout the project and ensured that the proposed solution remained focused on different user requirements.

### Persona considerations included:

* Digital confidence
* Accessibility needs
* Privacy and security expectations
* Healthcare management needs
* Communication requirements
* Navigation difficulties
* User independence

---

## 🗺️ Customer Journey Mapping

A customer journey map was created to understand the experience of users throughout their interaction with the PatientCare platform.

The journey considered:

**Awareness → Registration → Dashboard Navigation → Appointment Booking → Healthcare Resources → Communication → Outcome**

For each stage, the project considered:

* User actions
* Thoughts and feelings
* Pain points
* Proposed PatientCare solutions

This helped identify opportunities to improve accessibility, navigation, communication, and user confidence.

## 🧪 Usability Testing

Usability testing was conducted with **6 participants** using questions designed to evaluate the prototype's usability and effectiveness.

The testing focused on:

* Navigation
* Accessibility
* Communication features
* Privacy
* Information clarity
* Technical difficulties
* Overall usability

### Testing Findings

The results identified several areas for improvement, including:

* Navigation and instructions were not always sufficiently clear.
* Some participants experienced technical difficulties and slow performance.
* Privacy and security remained important concerns.
* Accessibility presented challenges for users with lower digital skills or language barriers.

The feedback was incorporated into the design thinking process and used to support improvements to the sketches, prototype, usability, and interface design.

## 🧩 QA & Software Testing Perspective

Although this project was primarily focused on UX research and design, the research and usability testing process also provided opportunities to apply a **quality assurance mindset**.

The project involved identifying potential issues from a user's perspective and analysing how design and system behaviour could affect the overall user experience.

### Areas considered from a QA perspective:

* User flows and navigation
* Usability issues
* Accessibility considerations
* User requirements
* Potential edge cases
* Information clarity
* System reliability
* Performance concerns
* Privacy and security considerations
* User feedback and defect identification

This project strengthened my understanding of how **UX research, usability testing, and software quality** can work together throughout the product development lifecycle.
